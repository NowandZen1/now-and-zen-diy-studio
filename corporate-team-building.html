Private events

Corporate team building in Dayton

Give your team something to do with their hands and something to take home. Everyone sits at a table, builds a terrarium together, and talks while they work. No trust falls, no slideshows, just a real afternoon your team will still be talking about next week. In our Fire Blocks District studio, or we bring it to your office.

Why teams love it

A screen-free hour that actually lands

Most team building asks people to perform. This asks them to make something. The pressure drops the moment hands get busy, and the conversation that follows is the real point. Coworkers who never talk end up trading plant tips and swapping stories.

  • No experience needed, everyone starts even
  • Screen-free and phone-down by nature
  • Quiet folks and loud folks both find a place in it
  • Everyone leaves with something they made
How it works
How it works

Four simple steps

We handle the setup, the materials, and the guiding. Your team just shows up and builds.

1

Tell us your group

Send us your headcount, your date, and whether you want to come to us or have us come to you.

2

We set the table

Vessels, plants, soil, sand, moss, and embellishments are set out and ready before your team walks in.

3

Everyone builds

A host walks the whole group through it, start to finish. Nobody gets left behind, and nobody has to be crafty.

4

Take it back to the desk

Everyone leaves with a finished terrarium for their desk, and a good story about the afternoon.
