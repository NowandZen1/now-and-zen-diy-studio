Terrarium DIY Kits in Dayton

Everything in a box to build a terrarium at home, or to give as a gift. Pick up the calm of the studio without leaving the couch.

What it is

The studio, in a box

A terrarium DIY kit is the whole build packed up to go. Think of it the way you would a meal kit, everything portioned and ready, with nothing to hunt down at the store. Open the box, follow the card, and you have a terrarium you made yourself.

Kits are our easiest gift. They land well for birthdays, thank yous, and hostess gifts, and they are just as good for a quiet night in at home.

  • Everything measured out and ready
  • Simple steps, no experience needed
  • Packs and ships without the plants spilling
  • Made and stocked here in Dayton
In the box
What is included

Everything you need to build

Each kit comes with the pieces and a simple set of steps, so you can build start to finish at home.

The build

Vessel, base, and plants

A vessel to build in, base layers to fill it, and the plants to set inside, all portioned and packed.

Add-ons

Accents and extras

Finishing touches like stones, moss, and small accents to make the build your own.

The steps

Simple instructions

A clear, step by step card so anyone can build with confidence, no green thumb required.

Who it is for

An easy gift, an easy night in

Kits are the pick when you want to give something a little more thoughtful than a card, or when you want a low key project at home. There is a size and a price band for most occasions, from a small grab and go gift to a bigger centerpiece build.

Not sure which one? Come in and we will help you match a kit to the person, or start with an air plant grab bag if you want something simple.

  • Beginner friendly kits for first time builders
  • Gift ready sizes for birthdays and thank yous
  • Air plant grab bags for a low fuss option
  • Larger kits for a centerpiece build
