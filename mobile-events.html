Private events

Mobile craft event in Dayton

Can't come to us? We come to you. We pack the Terrarium Bar into the van and bring the full build experience to your office, backyard, or venue anywhere around Dayton. Same plants, same materials, same guided instruction, set up in your space. It is a terrarium event we bring to you, and your group never has to leave the room.

How mobile works

The whole studio, packed into the van

We load up the vessels, plants, soil, sand, moss, and every embellishment, then set the Terrarium Bar up wherever you are. A host runs the build the same way we do in studio, so your group gets the full experience without the drive downtown.

  • We bring the Terrarium Bar to your location
  • Same materials and full plant selection
  • Same guided, host-led build
  • We set up and pack out, you just gather your people
