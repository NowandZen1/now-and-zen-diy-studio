Private events

Date night ideas in Dayton

Skip the dinner and a movie loop. Sit down together, drink in hand, and build a terrarium side by side in the Fire Blocks District of downtown Dayton. It is one of the easier things to do for couples in Dayton who want to actually talk, make something, and take a little of the night home. Just as good for a bachelorette group, a birthday, or any reason to celebrate.

Why it makes a good date

Make something together, not just small talk

A build gives you both something to do with your hands and something easy to talk about. The pressure of a first date eases up, and a long-time couple gets a night that is not the same old spot. Drink in hand, good music, and a finished piece to put on the shelf at home.

  • Sit close and build side by side
  • Bring a glass of wine to the table
  • Right on Third Street in the Fire Blocks District
  • Two vessels to take home, one for each of you

How the night goes
How the night goes

Come in, sit down, build

No experience needed. Everything is set out and ready, and a host walks you through it.

1

Settle in

Grab your seats, get a drink, and take in the plant wall. No rush, the night is yours.

2

Choose your pieces

Pick your vessel, plants, and embellishments from the Terrarium Bar along the wall.

3

Build side by side

Sit down at a table and create. A host is there the whole time if you want a hand.

4

Take it home

Leave with a piece each, a little reminder of the night on the shelf.
