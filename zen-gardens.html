Build a Zen Garden in Dayton

Our signature calm in a dish. Layered sand, smooth stones, and a few small plants, arranged into something grounding and simple. Build one at the studio or take one home.

What it is

A little stillness on the shelf

A Zen Garden is our take on the classic sand and stone garden, softened with living plants. Sand and fine gravel get layered into a shallow dish, then stones, air plants, and small greenery are set in place for a look that feels calm the moment you see it.

Each one is arranged by hand, so no two look quite the same. It is the piece people reach for when they want something quiet and grounding rather than lush and leafy.

  • Layered sand and fine gravel base
  • Smooth stones and natural accents
  • Air plants or small greenery
  • Set in a shallow, open dish

Sand and stone

Make it yours

Add crystals, small figures, or other extras from the wall to give your garden a bit of personality.

Who it is for

A calm gift, or a calm desk

Zen Gardens make an easy gift for the person who could use a little quiet, and they hold up well on a desk, a windowsill, or a bedside table. There is no watering schedule to keep up with and nothing to fuss over.

They are a good pick for a low key visit to the studio too, when you want to make something with your hands without a lot of steps.

  • Gifts for a housewarming, a thank you, or just because
  • A grounding piece for a desk or workspace
  • A calm, low fuss build for first timers
  • Simple to keep, easy to enjoy
