What to expect

Here is how a build goes, start to finish. It is easygoing, and you never have to know what you are doing before you walk in.

  • You arrive and get settled with a drink in hand.
  • You choose your plants and embellishments from the Terrarium Bar along the wall.
  • You sit down at a table with everything you picked.
  • A team member guides you the whole time while you create.
  • You take your finished terrarium home the same day.

There is a $10.00 base layer fee for terrarium creations. Bringing your own vessel? There is an additional fee ranging from $4 to $15 depending on vessel size.

Glass vessels, air plants, succulents, ferns and embellishments are all priced as marked. You control the cost of your design, and our experienced staff are there to guide you.

When you're done, bring your design to the check-out desk. We'll add up your plants, vessel, $10 layer fee, and any embellishments. Your terrarium will be boxed with a plant care sheet for the ride home.

We also have Zen Picks, set price terrarium builds at $20 and $32. Join us for a self-care day alone or bring a friend.
