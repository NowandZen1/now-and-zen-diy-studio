Option B

Cinematic film — full-bleed Remotion backdrop

Immersive. The walk-in becomes a wide, light-graded film: a soft blurred wash fills the frame, the real clip floats sharp in the center with a slow push-in, and the headline overlays it. Built in Remotion (video-as-code) so grade, motion, and framing are produced, not accidental. A richer "wow," a fixed rendered asset.

On the live site this film would play once, then cross-fade to the existing lifestyle photo slideshow, exactly the "video that flips to stills" idea from the 7/17 review.
