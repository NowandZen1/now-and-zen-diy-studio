Private events

Kids birthday parties in Dayton

A craft birthday party that stays fun and stays easy. Every kid builds a terrarium with guided help the whole way through, so nobody gets stuck and everybody finishes something they are proud of. The grown ups who came along get complimentary drinks and a seat to enjoy the chaos. You bring the birthday kid, we handle the mess.

Why parents book it

Guided the whole time, and easy on you

You do not have to plan an activity, run the activity, or clean up after it. A host leads every kid through the build step by step, keeps the pace moving, and handles the plants and soil so you do not have to. Affordable, hands-on, and the party favor is something they actually keep.

  • A host guides every kid start to finish
  • Affordable and hands-on the whole time
  • Complimentary drinks for the grown ups
  • Each kid takes home the terrarium they built
